What We Do

Our Programs

From helping families achieve homeownership to emergency repairs that keep people safely housed — we're building a stronger Horry County together.

Homeownership Program

An 18–24 month program where families complete monthly financial classes, 300+ hours of sweat equity, and purchase their Habitat home with an interest-free mortgage.

  • New home construction
  • Monthly financial literacy classes
  • 300+ hours sweat equity per adult
  • Zero-percent interest mortgage
Apply Today

Critical Home Repair

For low-income homeowners who can't afford urgent repairs, we step in. From leaky roofs to broken HVAC systems, our repair program keeps vulnerable families — especially seniors and those with disabilities — safely in their homes.

  • Roof repair & replacement
  • HVAC & plumbing systems
  • Accessibility modifications
  • Safety & weatherization
Request Repair Help

Habitat ReStore

Our two ReStore locations sell donated home improvement materials, appliances, and furniture at a fraction of retail prices. Shop here, donate your reusable items, and directly fund our mission — while keeping thousands of tons out of landfills.

  • Free donation pickup
  • Tax-deductible donations

Who We Are

A Part of a Global Movement,
Right Here at Home

Habitat for Humanity of Horry County is an affiliate of Habitat for Humanity International, one of the world's most recognized nonprofits. Since 1990, we've been working side-by-side with families, volunteers, and partners to build and repair safe, affordable homes in the Grand Strand and surrounding communities.

We believe that every family deserves a decent place to live. That's not just a slogan — it's the reason we show up every day with hammers, paintbrushes, and open hearts.

Our Mission

Seeking to put God's love into action, Habitat for Humanity brings people together to build homes, communities, and hope.

Our Vision

A world where everyone has a decent place to live — starting with every family in Horry County.

Our Values

Dignity, respect, partnership, and the belief that working together — neighbors helping neighbors — is how communities thrive.
